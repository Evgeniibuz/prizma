Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

PULSΞ ("we", "the terminal") is an AI market-intelligence product. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the service.

What we collect

  • Account — username and a hashed password (bcrypt). We never store passwords in plaintext.
  • Wallet — if you link a Solana wallet, we store its public address and a verification timestamp. We never request or hold your private keys or seed phrase; signing is read-only and authorizes no transactions.
  • Activity — basic event logs (login, logout, actions), IP address, and user-agent, used for security and abuse prevention.
  • Usage data — agent prompts, missions, and chat you submit, stored to provide history and improve responses.

What we do NOT collect

  • Private keys, seed phrases, or custody of your funds.
  • Off-platform browsing or third-party tracking profiles.

Third-party services

To provide market data and AI features we send necessary requests to providers such as CoinGecko, DexScreener, an LLM provider, and a Solana RPC endpoint (for public balance reads). These providers process data under their own policies. Public on-chain data (wallet balances, transactions) is inherently public.

How we use data

  • To authenticate you and resolve your $PLSX hold-to-access tier.
  • To generate signals, breakdowns, and agent missions.
  • To secure the service and prevent abuse.

Retention

Account and wallet records persist while your account is active. Generated signals are pruned automatically (older entries removed on a schedule). You can request deletion of your account and associated data.

Your choices

  • Unlink your wallet at any time from the terminal.
  • Request account deletion by contacting us.

Disclaimer

PULSΞ is an intelligence tool, not financial advice. Signals, scores, and AI output are informational. Crypto assets are volatile and you are responsible for your own decisions.
